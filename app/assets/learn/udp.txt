UDP — datagrams, no connection
==============================

  phone ── datagram ──▶ device      each send = one packet
  phone ◀── datagram ── device      may be lost, doubled,
                                    or arrive out of order

  [ IP header 20 ][ UDP header 8 ][ payload ≤ ~1472 on Wi-Fi ]
  UDP header: src port, dst port, length, checksum

Why use it
  • no handshake: first packet is data
  • message borders are kept (one send = one
    receive)
  • broadcast / multicast possible (mDNS)

What you must add yourself
  • reliability: ack + resend (CoAP CON)
  • ordering: sequence numbers
  • fragmentation: keep payloads small

Knobs
  host:port     where to send
  local port    0 = any; fix it when the device
                answers to a known port

Gotchas
  • Android drops multicast unless an app holds
    a multicast lock (this app does, for mDNS)
  • NAT/routers forget UDP "connections" after
    ~30 s of silence

Try
  CoAP tool → coap.me, then look at the UDP
  datagrams in Inside: one request, one reply.
