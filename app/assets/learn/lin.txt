LIN — the cheap single-wire bus in car bodies
=============================================

  one master (schedule), up to 16 slaves,
  one wire at 12 V, ≤ 20 kbit/s

  frame = header (master) + response (owner)
  break ≥13 bits low · sync 0x55 · PID
  (6-bit id + 2 parity bits) · 1–8 data ·
  checksum (classic: data only; enhanced:
  data + PID, LIN 2.x)

Used for windows, mirrors, seats — where CAN
is too expensive.

A UART can do LIN: the break is a long low,
sent by dropping the baud rate for one byte.

Not built yet: needs a TJA1021 transceiver on
the bridge UART. Commands would be
  lin init <baud> · lin send <pid> <hex>
  lin request <pid>
