UART — two wires, no clock
==========================

  idle ─┐ start ┌─┬─┬─┬─┬─┬─┬─┬─┐ (parity) stop ┌─ idle
        └───────┘ d0 ... d7 (LSB first)        └
  115200 8N1 = 115200 bit/s, 8 data, no parity,
  1 stop → 10 bits per byte → 11.5 kB/s

  TX ──▶ RX     both sides MUST agree on baud
  RX ◀── TX     and format beforehand
  GND ── GND    (always connect ground)

Levels
  3.3 V TTL (ESP32, STM32) — USB-UART adapters
  RS-232 ±12 V — needs a MAX3232, never wire
  straight to a microcontroller

Control lines
  DTR/RTS: ESP32 dev boards use them to reset
  into the bootloader (that is how flashing
  works); toggle them here to reset the board.

Phone side
  USB-OTG + CH340 / CP2102 / FTDI adapter,
  drivers from the community library
  usb-serial-for-android (mik3y).
  Bluetooth SPP (HC-05, ESP32 Classic BT)
  looks the same: a byte stream.

Gotchas
  • garbage text = wrong baud
  • nothing at all = TX/RX swapped
  • line endings: \n (Linux), \r\n (AT
    commands, Windows), \r (old modems)
