SPI — fast, full duplex, one chip select each
=============================================

  ESP32            device
  SCLK ──────────▶ SCK      clock from master
  MOSI ──────────▶ SDI/DI   master out
  MISO ◀────────── SDO/DO   master in
  CS   ──────────▶ CS (low = selected)

Every clock moves one bit each way: you always
receive as many bytes as you send. To read,
send dummy 0x00 bytes.

Modes (CPOL, CPHA)
  0  idle low,  sample on rising edge (most)
  1  idle low,  sample on falling edge
  2  idle high, sample on falling edge
  3  idle high, sample on rising edge

Example: SPI flash JEDEC id
  out 9F 00 00 00
  in  xx EF 40 18   (Winbond, 128 Mbit)

Gotchas
  • wrong mode = shifted or garbage bits
  • long wires + high clock = ringing; start
    at 1 MHz
  • no addresses: one CS line per device
