1-Wire — one data wire (plus ground), slow, cheap
=================================================

  3.3V ─ 4.7k ─┬── DQ ── DS18B20 ── DS18B20 ...
  ESP32 GPIO ──┘
  (devices can even be powered from DQ:
   "parasite power")

Time slots
  reset: master pulls low 480 µs, devices
         answer with a presence pulse
  write 1: short low · write 0: long low
  read:  short low, then sample

Every device has a unique 64-bit ROM:
  [family 1][serial 6][CRC-8 1]
  0x28 = DS18B20 temperature sensor
ROM search finds all devices bit by bit
(binary tree walk).

DS18B20
  Convert T (0x44) → wait 750 ms (12-bit)
  Read scratchpad (0xBE) → 9 bytes:
  temp LSB, MSB (1/16 °C, signed), TH, TL,
  config, 3 reserved, CRC-8
  0x0191 = 401/16 = 25.0625 °C

Gotchas
  • CRC fails → long wire or weak pull-up
  • 85.0 °C = power-on value: conversion
    did not run
