ESP-NOW — ESP32 to ESP32, no router
===================================

  ESP32 A ── Wi-Fi action frame ──▶ ESP32 B
  no association, no IP, no DHCP

  • up to 250 bytes per frame (v1)
  • addressed by MAC; ff:ff:ff:ff:ff:ff = all
  • optional encryption per peer (PMK/LMK)
  • range like Wi-Fi, latency ~ms
  • both sides on the same Wi-Fi channel

Good for: remote buttons, sensor nodes on
batteries (wake, send, sleep in ~100 ms).

Not: Internet, large data, phones (phones
cannot send ESP-NOW frames — that is why this
goes through the bridge).

Gotchas
  • if one side is also connected to a router,
    its channel follows the router
  • send callback "ok" means the peer's radio
    ACKed, not that the app got it
