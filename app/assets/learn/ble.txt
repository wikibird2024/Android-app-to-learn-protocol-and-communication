Bluetooth Low Energy — adverts, then GATT
=========================================

  1. Advertising (no connection)
     device shouts every 20 ms..10 s on
     channels 37/38/39: up to 31 bytes of
     AD structures [len][type][data]
       01 flags   09 name   03 16-bit UUIDs
       ff manufacturer data (company id first)

  2. Connection → GATT (attribute table)
     Service 180f Battery
       └ Characteristic 2a19 Battery Level
           properties: read, notify
           value: 0x5a (90 %)
           └ descriptor 2902 CCCD (notify on/off)

Operations
  read     phone asks, device answers
  write    with response (acked) or without
  notify   device pushes when it changes
           (phone wrote 01 00 to the CCCD)
  indicate notify + ack from the phone

Sizes
  ATT MTU default 23 → 20 bytes per value;
  this app asks for 247 after connecting.

UUIDs
  16-bit = SIG standard, short form of
  0000xxxx-0000-1000-8000-00805f9b34fb
  128-bit = vendor (Nordic UART 6e400001-...)

Gotchas
  • Android 12+: BLUETOOTH_SCAN / CONNECT
    permissions; older: location must be ON
  • phones hide raw advert bytes; this app
    rebuilds them from the parsed fields
  • ESP32 NimBLE example "bleprph" makes a
    good test peripheral
